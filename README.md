## Hi, I'm Imad

I like taking a messy problem and turning it into something useful.

Most of my projects are around AI tools, supplier data and business workflows. Sometimes I take a detour and make a game about loading containers onto ships.

### A few things I've made

- **[Frayproof](https://github.com/ImadC9/frayproof)** — checks what gets lost or broken when an AI agent's context is shortened, retried or handed off.
- **[Supplier Integrity Desk](https://github.com/ImadC9/supplier-integrity-desk)** — helps find duplicate suppliers and incomplete records, with the evidence there for someone to review.
- **[Sort the Shipment](https://github.com/ImadC9/sort-the-shipment)** — a harbour puzzle game made in Godot with AI assistance. [You can play it here](https://imadch.itch.io/sort-the-shipment), or [read how it came together](https://github.com/ImadC9/sort-the-shipment/blob/main/docs/case-study.md).
- **[light-keynote-html](https://github.com/ImadC9/light-keynote-html)** — an HTML presentation skill and template that grew out of an AI workshop deck.

I like simple interfaces, tools that explain their results, and being clear about what still needs work.

I also contribute to [Kaitai Struct](https://github.com/kaitai-io) and [ImHex Patterns](https://github.com/WerWolv/ImHex-Patterns), mostly around binary formats and parsing. My [pull requests](https://github.com/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AImadC9) are here if you're curious.
